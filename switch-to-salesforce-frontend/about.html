About Switch To Salesforce

A free learning platform built for anyone starting or growing their Salesforce career.

Switch To Salesforce is a structured, documentation-style learning platform covering Salesforce development and administration. Whether you are a developer transitioning from another tech stack, an admin looking to level up, a fresher preparing for your first role, or a professional getting ready for Salesforce interviews — this platform is built for you.

What you will find here

  • Step-by-step guides on Apex, Lightning Web Components, Flow Builder, and more
  • Salesforce fundamentals explained clearly — no buzzwords, no fluff
  • Real-world scenarios and interview preparation
  • Admin patterns, data modelling, security, and platform architecture
  • Certification roadmaps and structured learning paths

Who it is for

  • Developers transitioning into Salesforce from Java, .NET, Python, or other stacks
  • Salesforce administrators expanding into development
  • Freshers and graduates starting their Salesforce journey
  • Professionals preparing for Salesforce Admin, Developer, and Architect certifications
  • Anyone preparing for a Salesforce interview and wanting structured, practical guidance

Part of the Switch To Salesforce ecosystem

  • 📚 This platform (learn.switchtosalesforce.com) — free guides, tutorials, and structured learning paths.
  • 🎯 Mock Interview Prep (switchtosalesforce.com) — 1:1 mock interviews, personalised feedback, and a structured prep plan to crack your first Salesforce role.
  • 📝 Blog (blog.switchtosalesforce.com) — articles, case studies, and practical career notes for Salesforce professionals.
